# AsoHub: Business Brief

## The business
AsoHub is a made-to-measure tailoring studio with a customer app for orders, measurements and payments. The studio makes event outfits, corporate wear, wedding sets and alterations. The owner works with a small team of seamstresses and apprentices.

## The person who uses it
Customers are men and women aged 25 to 45, mostly working professionals and event-goers. They use Android phones on mobile data that is often patchy. They are comfortable with banking apps and WhatsApp, but they are not technical. The owner and staff use a phone and a shared tablet in the shop.

## The one thing that must work
A customer sees the true status of their outfit and exactly what they still owe. The studio never loses a measurement or a payment record.

## Three questions only this business can answer
1. Will my outfit be ready before my event?
2. What have I paid, and what do I still owe?
3. Can you reuse my measurements from last time, or must I come back in?

## How the interface must feel
Calm, precise and warm. Customers hand over money and body measurements, so trust comes first. The interface should feel like a well-cut garment: clean lines, generous space, no clutter.

This drives the type. A refined serif for display headlines gives the brand craft and character. A clear sans for body text, amounts and dates keeps them readable on a small screen in daylight. A loud or playful interface would signal carelessness with money, so it is the wrong direction.
